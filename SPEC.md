# Project: Premium Web Design & Development Studio

I want to create a complete, production-quality website for a new web design and development business.

The business will sell beautifully designed websites to small businesses, entrepreneurs, restaurants, and independent professionals. Beyond traditional websites, we will also offer custom functionality, applications, automations, integrations, and almost anything a customer can imagine their website doing.

The business should be designed to operate with minimal direct client interaction. Customers should be able to explore designs, understand pricing, describe their requirements, and eventually order and pay online.

**Our central message:**

"Beautiful websites. Limitless possibilities."

**Our primary homepage headline:**

"A beautiful website is just the beginning."

**Supporting copy:**

"We design exceptional websites. Then we make them do whatever your business needs."

## 1. Design direction

This is extremely important.

I want an exceptionally beautiful, sophisticated, contemporary website that feels like an award-winning independent digital design studio.

Design references should come from high-end editorial design, architecture studios, premium creative agencies, and sophisticated digital product companies.

The website should feel expensive, confident, and visually distinctive.

Design principles:

- Strong, oversized editorial typography
- Beautifully considered whitespace
- Precise alignment and consistent spacing
- Restrained colour palette
- Primarily dark charcoal, off-white, and carefully selected accent colours
- Sophisticated typography pairing
- Subtle animations and refined transitions
- Large, immersive project previews
- Interesting but disciplined layouts
- Exceptional mobile experience
- Original visual identity

Avoid:

- Generic SaaS website layouts
- Overused AI-generated design aesthetics
- Purple gradients and glowing backgrounds
- Generic stock photography
- Excessive rounded cards
- Floating glassmorphism panels
- Emoji-heavy design
- Fake testimonials, clients, or awards
- Excessive marketing language
- Generic illustrations
- Anything resembling a cheap website template marketplace

The design must have a strong personality while remaining elegant and accessible.

## 2. Homepage

Build a complete homepage with the following sections.

### Hero

Large, visually striking typography:

"A beautiful website is just the beginning."

Supporting copy:

"We design exceptional websites. Then we make them do whatever your business needs."

Two calls to action:

"Explore websites"

"Imagine what's possible"

Include an impressive visual treatment that immediately demonstrates our design capabilities.

### Selected websites

Create a beautiful portfolio-style section featuring four initial website concepts:

01 — The Restaurant

02 — The Studio

03 — The Boutique

04 — The Professional

Each concept should have its own distinct visual identity and realistic, polished demo homepage.

These should not simply be four identical layouts with different photographs.

Each should look like an independently designed, premium website.

Visitors should eventually be able to open interactive live previews and choose a design to purchase.

Clearly identify these as demonstration concepts rather than completed client projects.

### Beyond the website

Introduce our custom development capabilities.

Headline:

"What if your website could do more?"

Supporting copy:

"Reservations. Payments. Appointments. Customer portals. Automations. Or something nobody's built before."

Present these capabilities in an elegant, interactive format.

### Interactive functionality explorer

Create an interactive section asking:

"What do you want your website to do?"

Allow visitors to select:

- Take reservations
- Accept payments
- Book appointments
- Sell products
- Manage customers
- Generate quotes
- Create customer accounts
- Automate repetitive tasks
- Connect existing software
- Something entirely different

Selections should be stored in application state and used to prefill the custom project inquiry.

The interaction should be polished, fast, and intuitive.

### The imagination section

This should be one of the most memorable sections of the website.

Large headline:

"What if your website could..."

Below it, create an elegant, prominent text input where visitors can describe anything they want their website to accomplish.

Placeholder:

"Automatically take bookings, send confirmations, and remember my customers..."

Button:

"Explore your idea"

For the initial version, create a functional requirements-intake flow that captures the idea and allows the visitor to proceed to a structured project inquiry.

Architect this so an AI-powered project analysis and preliminary estimate can be added later, but do not present invented AI estimates or pretend that an AI service is connected.

### Pricing

Create two clearly differentiated offerings.

**01 — Beautiful by design**

Websites from $999 CAD.

Includes:
- Professional customisation of a selected design
- Up to five pages
- Mobile-responsive development
- Basic SEO
- Contact form

## 4. Ordering and project intake

Design a clean, multi-step project intake process.

For standard websites, collect:

- Selected website design
- Business name
- Business category
- Contact information
- Existing website, if any
- Desired pages
- Logo and brand assets
- Preferred colours
- Content and image availability
- Domain ownership
- Additional requirements

For custom development, collect:

- Project description
- Desired functionality
- Intended users
- Required integrations
- Budget range
- Desired timeline

The intake should save progress and show a clear summary before submission.

Do not require a phone call or meeting.

Do not implement live payment processing until the order flow and scope are finalized.

## 5. Technology

Use:

- Next.js with App Router
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Supabase for database and file storage when needed
- Stripe for eventual checkout and payments
- Vercel-compatible deployment

Use a clean, maintainable component architecture.

Implement responsive layouts, accessible controls, form validation, appropriate loading states, and sensible SEO metadata.

Avoid unnecessary dependencies.

Use environment variables for secrets. Never expose service-role credentials or private API keys in client-side code.

## 6. Business model considerations

This is intended to be a small, profitable business operated by one person.

The site should encourage self-service purchasing and minimize sales calls, meetings, repetitive questions, and manual administration.

The website should eventually support:

- Customer accounts
- Order status tracking
- Asset uploads
- Project approval
- Revision requests
- Online payments
- Maintenance subscriptions
- Automated transactional emails

These are future capabilities. Design the architecture with them in mind, but prioritize a polished and functional initial release.

Do not overengineer the first version.

## 7. Brand identity

We have not chosen a final business name.

Use a tasteful temporary wordmark that can be easily replaced.

Create a cohesive visual identity with a recognizable typographic style.

The brand should communicate:

- Creativity
- Technical sophistication
- Trust
- Simplicity
- Independence
- Exceptional design

Avoid making AI the central selling point. AI may be part of our internal workflow, but customers are purchasing design quality, technical capability, and a completed product.

## 8. Build priorities

Start with:

1. Project setup and architecture
2. Global visual design system
3. Complete homepage
4. Four visually distinct demo concepts
5. Interactive functionality explorer
6. Pricing and service pages
7. Project intake flow
8. Responsive and accessibility refinements

Create working components rather than static mockups.

Use realistic demonstration content, clearly labelled where appropriate.

Do not invent client testimonials, portfolio projects, awards, or business performance claims.

## 9. Quality expectations

This must not look like a website generated from a standard AI prompt.

Make thoughtful design decisions.

Use typography, composition, interaction, and restraint to create something genuinely distinctive.

Prioritize visual quality over the number of features.

Ensure that the website looks exceptional on both desktop and mobile.

Build the initial version, run the available checks, and report any incomplete functionality or configuration requirements.

**Begin by creating the project foundation and a complete, visually polished homepage. Then proceed through the remaining features in manageable stages.**
